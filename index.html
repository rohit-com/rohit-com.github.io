Features

Everything a chat needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for real conversations — not another inbox to manage.

Search by username

No numbers, no contacts to import. Find anyone by the name they chose and start talking.

Message requests

The first message goes as a request. They accept, decline, or just reply — never an unwanted inbox.

Reactions & typing

React, edit, unsend, and watch the typing dots — small details that make it feel alive.

Privacy, your way

Turn off read receipts or activity status any time. Only your username is ever shown.

Installs like an app

Add it to your home screen — its own icon, its own window, no browser bar.

Themes per chat

Set a different mood for every conversation — solid palettes or full illustrated scenes.
